About REON Energies

REON Energies Private Limited is a trusted solar energy solutions provider and EPC company delivering reliable, affordable, and sustainable solar energy solutions for homes, businesses, industries, and institutions across India.

We specialize in complete solar project execution — from design and procurement to installation and commissioning — with a focus on quality, transparency, and long-term customer satisfaction.

Our Core Specializations:

  • Solar Panels
  • Solar Inverters
  • Battery Storage Systems
  • ACDB & DCDB
  • Combiner Boxes
  • Solar Installation
  • On-Grid Systems
  • Off-Grid Systems
  • Hybrid Systems
  • Residential Projects
  • Commercial Projects
  • Industrial Projects
